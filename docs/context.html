Why Context Matters

Traditional dictionaries give you static definitions. But words change meaning based on context. "Bank" means something different in finance vs. geography. "Cell" means something different in biology vs. telecommunications.

How Aletheia Works

When you select a word or phrase, Aletheia sends both the selection and the surrounding text to understand the context. This allows the AI to provide explanations that are relevant to what you're actually reading.
